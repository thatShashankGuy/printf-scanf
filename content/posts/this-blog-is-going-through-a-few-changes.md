This blog is going through a few changes.

I have been thinking about writing this for a while. Reading my blogs from this year, you must have seen a shift in content. Most of the writeups are not about the how-tos and whats of technology but mostly my opinions about it. Most of my energy went to communicating my feelings and understanding working with AI. I do not want to make this blog all about AI, but currently that is what I spend most of my time with.

I started this blog in 2023 with the goal of documenting my own learning and producing technical writeups to help others. I was inspired by a lot of blogs from the 2010s when I learned programming. I used to read a lot of blogs because a lot of official documentation was a bit much for my newbie brain.

That is what blogs are great for: distilling dense information and presenting it in a more digestible format. This is gen AI's best use case. Not only can it distill information, but also break down a segment of a large document. In my opinion, doing the same manually is not a good use of this blog.

Regarding learning, I have created a new workflow. I have always preferred HTML docs over markdown notes. HTML is more readable and you can create segments, flash cards, and highlighted areas in a cleaner way than MD files. The only caveat of maintaining a repository of HTML docs is that HTML is terrible to write. MD is a nice compromise because it is okay to read and amazing to write.

But AI has solved that problem too. I can write raw notes while I am learning and then feed them to AI to generate neat HTML files with the exact layout needed to learn. This is further automated by adding a skill in Claude with tailor-made instructions to keep the consistency of all notes as well as use layout and design to suit my specific needs. I will write a separate blog on Agent Skills to expand on this. I maintain this repo separately and all my notes can be accessed at [here](https://shashankshekhar.in).

What happens to this site? What is the best use of this blog? Well, AI at this time generates more than half of internet blogs. According to a [recent study by Graphite](https://graphite.io/five-percent/research/more-articles-are-now-created-by-ai-than-humans), more than 50% of articles on the web are now generated by AI, a trend captured in detail by [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/more-than-half-of-new-articles-on-the-internet-are-being-written-by-ai-is-human-writing-headed-for-extinction-268354). At this point of time, having your own voice is extremely important. I want to use this platform to do just that, something you have seen this year with my critiques and praise of Gen AI, LLM, and software engineering. I will keep talking about technologies but will also add more to the mix. I also have a few interesting historical facts and ideas discussed elsewhere. This keeps my head clear. I will use this blog as an open journal.

This is also something I think I will lose if I keep writing technical how-tos: my own way of thinking and writing. How-tos are extremely easy to generate from AI, but your own thought process is not. ChatGPT can write a 1000-word article on neural networks, but struggles writing how NLP changed my mind about the capabilities of language, precisely how I want.

Use of AI: yes, this blog will have light editing by AI, but the words will be my own. So expect incoherent tangents.

I am excited for this new phase of this blog and I am sure you are too. See you next time.
